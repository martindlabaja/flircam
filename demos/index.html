flircam demos

Mask-driven visuals. Runs in Chrome on Windows; served from WSL.

Thawing snow Snow falls over everything; the silhouette melts it down to wet ground that slowly dries. Ping-pong height field + heat diffusion. Fireflies A swarm that hangs in the dark, wakes up near the body and orbits it, leaving glowing trails. Grid-cell particles steering on a diffused mask field. Flowering meadow Grass until the body touches it; flowers open in the wake, deepen in colour as they age, and slowly wilt back. Bloom field + procedural petals. Plasma — shadertoy A shadertoy shader run as-is: the harness supplies iResolution / iTime / iChannel0, the silhouette warps the field. Ink — shadertoy + Buffer A Two-pass shadertoy toy: the body lays down ink, a feedback buffer drags it along a curl field and lets it fade.

Keys inside a demo: h panel · f fullscreen · r reset · space pause · s back here. Shaders hot-reload on save.
